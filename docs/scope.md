# Project Scope

## In Scope

### User Management

- Registration
- Authentication & Password Reset (via transactional security emails)
- Authorization
- User Profile Management
- Account Deletion & Permanent Data Purging

---

### Financial Management

- Income Tracking
- Expense Tracking
- Categories (Default System Categories & Custom Categories with Reassignment)
- Transaction History (Date, Merchant/Payee, Notes, Backdating, Dynamic Recalculations)

---

### Budget Management

- Monthly Budgets per Category
- Budget Monitoring & Warning Threshold Indicators (80% approach & 100%+ exceed warnings)

---

### Savings

- Savings Goals (Create, Edit, Delete)
- Contribution & Progress Tracking

---

### Dashboard

- Financial Summary (Net Balance calculation supporting positive and negative balances)
- Charts
- Analytics
- Recent Activity

---

### User Experience

- Responsive Design
- Dark Mode / Theme Toggle
- Search & Filtering
- Currency Symbol Display Preference (Global formatting without exchange rate conversion)

---

# Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from Version 1.0:

- Marketing and reminder email notifications (Transactional security emails for password reset are In Scope)
- Multi-currency exchange rate conversion (Visual currency display symbol formatting is In Scope)
- Bank synchronization
- Credit card integration
- AI financial recommendations
- OCR receipt scanning
- Investment portfolio management
- Shared family accounts
- Payroll management
- Multi-language support
- Native mobile applications
- Cryptocurrency wallets
- Tax calculation
- Bill reminders
- SMS notifications
- Offline mode

These features may be considered in future releases after the MVP is complete.
